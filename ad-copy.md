# Scalevertise - Meta Ad Copy

Built on Hormozi's GOATed Ads framework (50 hooks across awareness levels × meat × CTA) and the agency's "Building The Ads System" doc (direct response hook → proof → educational angle, optimize for booked calls).

**Audience:** Care home owners / administrators, UK & US. Mostly **Problem-Aware** (they know beds are empty, don't know paid ads fix it) and **Solution-Aware** (know marketing exists, haven't found one that works for care homes).

**Core offer (in every ad's body):** We get care homes 20 to 30 qualified resident appointments in 30 days, or you don't pay our fee.

**Proof to lean on (honest, early-stage process proof):** funnels built from scratch generating qualified leads at ~$20 (QuackQuotes) and ~$24 (Subutai AI). Frame as "the system already produces cheap, qualified leads" - never claim care-home revenue we don't have yet.

## PART 2 - THE MEAT (3 full ad scripts)

Pair any hook above with one of these bodies. Keep them direct-to-camera or simple text-on-screen.

### Ad #1 - Story / Problem-Solution (best all-rounder)

> [HOOK] Your beds aren't empty because of your home. They're empty because the families looking for care right now can't find you.
>
> Here's what's happening. The sons and daughters searching for a place for mum or dad are on Facebook tonight, stressed, and ready to act. But they don't know your home exists. So they call the first home that shows up, and book with whoever replies first.
>
> We fix that. We run targeted ads that put your home in front of those exact families, capture their details, and follow up within minutes so they book a tour straight onto your calendar. Not a list of names. Booked, qualified appointments.
>
> And we guarantee it: 20 to 30 qualified resident appointments in 30 days, or you don't pay our fee.
>
> We only work with one home per area. [CTA]

Two: they hit a page that pre-qualifies them, so you're not wasting time on tyre-kickers. Three: every lead gets contacted fast and booked onto your calendar before they go cold.

> That's the difference. Instead of being one of ten listings someone might find, you get your own predictable pipeline of appointments. Predictable input, predictable output.
>
> We're so confident it works, we guarantee 20 to 30 appointments in 30 days or you don't pay our fee. [CTA]

### Ad #3 - Direct Offer (for warm / retargeting audiences)

> [HOOK] We get care homes 20 to 30 qualified resident appointments in 30 days. Or you don't pay our fee.

> Done-for-you ads. Instant follow-up. Booked tours on your calendar. You own the ad account, we run everything.

## PART 3 - CTAs (clear > clever)

Test these on identical hook+meat combos, change only the CTA.

1. **Click below and answer a few quick questions. If your area is open, we'll show you exactly how it works.**
2. **Tap "Learn More" to check if your area is still available. We only take one home per area.**
3. **Click below to see if we can fill your beds. 24-hour response, no long sales pitch.**
4. **Check your area now, before a competing home takes it.**

**Show-and-tell version (Hormozi's upgrade):** end the video by literally showing the next screen - "you'll land here, answer five quick questions about your home, and we'll come back to you within a day." Congruence lifts conversion.

## NOTES FOR THE AD ACCOUNT

- **Objective:** Leads (or Booked Call) to start, per the agency's Ads System doc. Move to "Schedule" optimization only after you have appointment data.
- **Targeting:** Start broad (top-of-market) + layer the niche interests the agency suggests. Don't hyper-narrow early.
- **Creative split:** 10 hooks per 1 piece of meat (Hormozi). Record once, chop into many.
- **Speed-to-lead is the whole game** (from your Appointment Setting doc): every lead called within 5 minutes, ideally under 60 seconds. The ads only work if the follow-up is instant.
- **Compliance:** no medical/clinical claims. Speak to the family's search for a safe, caring home, not health outcomes.
