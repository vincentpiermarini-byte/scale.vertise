Section 01

What Happened & Why the CPL Is High

Three separate things hit at the same time which made it hard to see what was actually causing the problem. Here's the honest breakdown.

IssueImpactWhat actually happened
Account pause (4 days) Major When a Meta account pauses due to a payment issue, the algorithm's learning phase resets completely. Meta needs 7 to 14 days of uninterrupted spend to figure out who to show the ads to. The pause wiped that progress right as the campaign was starting to optimize. This alone can double CPL for 1 to 2 weeks after restarting.
Static-only creative Major For home services like pressure washing, video dramatically outperforms static images. The transformation is visual and emotional — it needs to be seen in motion to stop the scroll. The before/after statics we ran were a starting point but not where we should stay long term.
Offer needs a real deal Medium "Free sidewalk wash" is a nice add-on but it doesn't make someone stop and think "I need to book this now." The new offer gives a concrete dollar saving on the bundle which gives people a real reason to act.
Lead follow-up speed Major See Section 02. This is the piece that explains why the 3 leads didn't respond — it's not a lead quality problem.
The good news

All of these are fixable. The account pause is a one-time setback and the algorithm will re-learn. The creative and offer changes are ready to go as soon as the videos are filmed. The follow-up system takes about 20 minutes to set up. We're not starting from scratch — we're making the right adjustments at the right time.

Section 02

Why Those 3 Leads Didn't Respond

This one is important to understand because it changes how we think about "lead quality." The leads that didn't pick up weren't bad leads. They were warm leads that went cold in the time between submitting the form and getting a call back. Here's the data on why that happens:

21x
More likely to close a lead contacted within 5 minutes vs. 30 minutes
80%
Drop in conversion rate when follow-up happens after 30 minutes
60 sec
How long a motivated lead stays "hot" before Facebook shows them the next ad

Here's what happens in practice. Someone sees the ad, they're in the moment, they fill out the form. While they're waiting to hear back, they keep scrolling. A competitor's ad shows up. That company has an auto-text that fires in 30 seconds. The homeowner responds to them instead. By the time we call, they've already got a quote from someone else and they don't even remember filling out our form.

This isn't about the ads — it's about the speed of the system around the ads. The fix is an automated text message that fires the second someone submits, so they feel taken care of immediately even if you're on a job.

Instant auto-text to set up (fires within 60 seconds of form submit)

"Hey [First Name]! It's Josh from Blue Wave Pro Wash. Just saw your request come through — I'm pulling together your quote now and will give you a call shortly. Feel free to reply here if you have any questions in the meantime!"

This gets set up through a simple Zapier connection between the Facebook lead form and a texting tool like GoHighLevel or even just a basic SMS automation. Takes about 20 minutes to configure. Once it's live, every lead gets contacted instantly without you having to do anything.

Bottom line

  1. Running ads without instant follow-up is like having a great store with nobody at the counter. The ads can be perfect and we'll still lose jobs.
  2. Getting this text automation live is the single highest ROI thing we can do right now, separate from any creative changes.
  3. Once it's running, the "lead didn't respond" problem goes away.
Section 03

The New Offer — Bundle & Save

The old offer (free sidewalk or porch add-on) was an extra, not a reason to book now. The new offer gives people a real dollar amount they're saving when they book the package, which creates genuine urgency without a fake countdown timer.

The New Bundle Offer

House Wash + Driveway — Save $150 When You Book This Month

Book both in one visit and we take $150 off the total. One trip. Done the same day. We only take a limited number of new clients each month in Cary, Apex, Raleigh, and Pittsboro — so this deal disappears when the spots fill up.

The $150 discount is real, simple to explain, and easy for the homeowner to understand. It makes the bundle feel like the obvious choice vs. booking each service separately.

Why this works better than the old offer:

  • Concrete saving — "$150 off" is tangible. "Free sidewalk wash" makes people wonder what it's actually worth.
  • Higher job value — house + driveway in one visit means a bigger ticket per booking, which offsets the discount immediately.
  • Real urgency — limited monthly spots is believable for a local owner-operator. It's true and it doesn't feel gimmicky.
  • Easy to say on camera — "book the package, save $150" is one clear sentence that lands in a UGC video.
How to present the pricing on the form

Don't show a specific price on the ad or form since every house is different. Just communicate the $150 saving on the bundle. When you follow up you give them the actual quote and the discount applies to the total. Simple.

Section 05

UGC Video Scripts — Film These on a Job Site

Phone camera. On location. Talk like you're telling a neighbor, not filming a commercial. These hooks are built on Hormozi's framework — statement and question hooks that make the viewer say "wait, that's me" in the first two seconds. The awareness level we're targeting is problem-aware: they know their driveway is dirty, they just haven't acted. So we make them feel it first, then give them the easy out.

Script 1 · Statement Hook — "That's me" recognition — film anywhere, talking head — approx. 65 sec
Hook — statement, say it slow, let it land

"Your driveway is making your house look older than it is. And you probably don't even notice anymore because it's happened so slowly."

Problem

"I pull up to houses every week where the homeowner has no idea how bad it's gotten. The concrete's almost black. Mold creeping up the siding. And they've just stopped seeing it. But their neighbors see it. Their guests see it."

Agitate

"And the thing is it's not even their fault. It's just one of those things you keep meaning to deal with and never do. Meanwhile it gets worse. And the longer you leave it the more the algae actually works into the surface and starts doing real damage."

Mechanism

"What we do is come out one time, wash your full house exterior and your driveway in the same visit, and you're done. Two to three hours. Most customers tell us they didn't realize how bad it had gotten until they see it clean. It's like a completely different house."

Offer

"Right now we're running a bundle deal — book the house and driveway together and save $150. We serve Cary, Apex, Raleigh, and Pittsboro and we only take a limited number of new clients a month."

CTA

"If you've been putting this off, tap below. Takes 60 seconds to get your quote and I'll get back to you same day."

Script 2 · Question Hook — pain-driven — film at job site with before/after visible — approx. 60 sec
Hook — direct question, pause after it

"Quick question. When's the last time you actually looked at your driveway? Not walked past it. Actually looked at it."

Problem

"Because most homeowners in Cary and Apex are losing curb appeal every single month and don't even realize it. The driveway, the siding, the walkways — that's the first thing a guest or a buyer sees before they even walk through the door. And if it looks rough, that's the impression your home makes."

Proof — hold up phone showing before/after or pan to the job

"This was a home we did right here last week. Before. After. Same day. That's what two and a half hours looks like."

Agitate

"I've had customers pull out their phones and look at old photos of their house and just go quiet. Because they forgot what it looked like when it was clean. It happened that gradually."

Offer

"Book the house and driveway together this month and we take $150 off. Limited spots in Cary, Apex, Raleigh, and Pittsboro."

CTA + Guarantee

"Tap below. 60 seconds for your quote. If you're not happy with the result we come back and redo it free."

Script 3 · Conditional Hook — "if this is you" format — talking head, casual — approx. 65 sec
Hook — conditional call-out

"If you own a home in Cary, Apex, Raleigh, or Pittsboro and you've been meaning to get your driveway and house washed — this one's for you."

Problem

"Because here's what I see constantly. People either put it off because they don't want to deal with finding someone reliable. Or they rent a pressure washer and spend a Saturday doing a mediocre job that looks okay for six weeks and then it's right back to where it was."

Agitate

"And the mold and algae that builds up isn't just ugly — it's breaking down the surface. One wrong pressure setting on concrete and you etch it permanently. I've seen it. It's expensive to fix."

Mechanism

"Here's what we do instead. One visit. Soft wash on the siding so nothing gets damaged. High pressure on the concrete where it's needed. House and driveway, done the same day, most jobs under three hours."

Offer

"This month we're doing a bundle deal — $150 off when you book both together. We cap how many new clients we take each month so quality stays high."

Guarantee + CTA

"If you're not completely happy with how it looks, we come back and redo it. No questions asked. Tap the link below, get your free quote in 60 seconds, I'll hit you back same day."

Book your house wash + driveway as a package this month and save $150.

One visit. Done the same day. Limited spots available.

Format: black text on white background, screenshot from your phone's Notes app. Do not design this. The lo-fi look is what makes it stop the scroll.

Static 2 — Dramatic Before/After Split

Image: Full-width split. Left: dark stained driveway. Right: bright clean concrete. Large bold text across the top: "SAME DRIVEWAY. 2 HOURS."

Small text on image: Blue Wave Pro Wash — Cary, Apex, Raleigh, Pittsboro

Caption: "This is what a 2-hour visit looks like. House wash + driveway bundle — save $150 when you book both this month. Limited spots."

Use the most dramatic before/after from a real job. The bigger the visual difference, the better it stops the scroll. Don't use a subtle clean — find the grossest before shot you have.
