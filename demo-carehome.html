Common Questions

The things families always ask us

How much does assisted living cost?

Monthly rates at Cedar Grove range from $4,200–$6,800 depending on care level and room type. We'll walk you through all pricing clearly, with no hidden fees and no surprises. Many families also access Veterans benefits, long-term care insurance, or state assistance programs we can help you navigate.

How do I know my loved one will be safe?

Cedar Grove is state licensed, regularly inspected, and staffed 24/7 by licensed nurses and certified aides. Our 1:6 caregiver ratio is above state minimum requirements. We also use a digital care monitoring system so every resident check-in is logged and visible to the family.

What if my loved one doesn't want to move?

This is the most common concern we hear. We've helped hundreds of families through this transition. Our care advisors are trained specifically in helping families navigate this conversation, and most residents surprise their families by settling in within the first two weeks.

How quickly can my loved one move in?

In many cases, we can facilitate a move within 5–10 business days once care assessment and paperwork are complete. If the situation is urgent, like a hospital discharge or a sudden decline, call us directly and we'll prioritize. We've moved residents in as fast as 48 hours.
