Why Beds Stay Empty

The Real Cost of Unpredictable Occupancy

Most care homes fill beds through hospital referrals and word of mouth — channels you don't control. When referrals slow, beds sit empty and revenue bleeds. Every day a bed goes unfilled is money you will never recover.

An Empty Bed Is a Daily Loss

Every vacant bed costs you $3,000 to $8,000 a month in lost revenue. That is not a projection — it is money leaving your business today, every day, while you wait for the next referral to arrive on its own schedule.

Referrals You Don't Control

Hospital discharge coordinators and placement agencies decide who hears about your home. When their priorities shift, your occupancy drops. You have no pipeline you own — and no way to predict what next month looks like.

Inquiries That Go Cold in Minutes

A family contacts several homes at once. Whoever follows up first gets the tour — and usually gets the placement. If your intake relies on a callback later that day, you are losing inquiries you will never know existed.

Tours That Don't Show Up

Even when a family agrees to visit, without a systematic confirmation and reminder process, no-shows are common. Your team's time, the family's trust, and your revenue expectation — all wasted when no one walks through the door.

How It Works

The Occupancy Engine

A purpose-built system for care homes. Every step is designed around one reality: a family researching care for a loved one makes their decision quickly, emotionally, and in favor of the home that reaches them first with warmth and clarity.

01

The ReachThe Family-Targeted Ad

We build Meta ads that speak directly to adult children and spouses actively searching for care options — at the moment the need is real and urgent. The ad doesn't sell your home. It acknowledges their situation and invites them to learn more, creating a warm, qualified inquiry rather than a cold click from the general public.

02

The CaptureOne-Tap Inquiry Form

We use Meta's pre-filled lead form — their name and number are already there. One tap to submit. No friction, no seven-field form, no reason to abandon. The simpler the ask, the more real families actually reach out. Every extra field is a family who chose another home instead.

03

The Lock-InInstant Follow-Up, Booked Tour

The moment a family submits, a warm, automated text goes out within seconds. It invites them to pick a visit time directly from your calendar. They book while the intent is present. A confirmation and reminder go out automatically. You get a scheduled tour — not a name in a spreadsheet for your staff to chase.

Frequently Asked Questions

We have tried marketing agencies before and got leads we could not convert. How is this different?

Most agencies hand you a list of names and call the job done. The gap between a submitted form and a family who actually walks through your door is where occupancy is won or lost — and most agencies do not close that gap. Our system follows up within seconds of every inquiry, books the tour automatically, and sends confirmations and reminders so families show up. We guarantee booked, showed tours — not leads. If we do not hit the agreed number, we refund our fee. You message us and we issue it. No process, no dispute.

Is Meta advertising appropriate for care homes? Will it reach the right families?

Yes — and it is one of the most underused channels in this sector. The families making care decisions are adults between 45 and 70, and they are on Facebook and Instagram. More importantly, they are targetable: Meta lets us reach people by location, life stage, and behavioral signals that indicate someone is actively researching care options for a family member. The ads we run do not feel like advertising — they acknowledge the family's situation with warmth and direct them to learn more. The result is an inquiry from someone already in decision mode, not a cold click from a casual browser.

What do you actually do, and how much of my time does this take?

We handle everything on the advertising and booking side: writing and designing the ads, building the inquiry form, setting up the instant text-back, and connecting to your calendar. You do one onboarding call — roughly 60 minutes — where we learn about your home, your capacity, and what a good resident fit looks like for you. After that, your only job is to show up for the tours we put on your calendar. We send a weekly report so you always know what is running and what it is producing.
