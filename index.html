Why Beds Stay Empty

The Real Cost of Unpredictable Occupancy

When referrals slow, beds sit empty. Every unfilled day is revenue you can't recover.

An Empty Bed Is a Daily Loss

Every vacant bed costs you $3,000 to $8,000 a month in lost revenue. That is not a projection; it is money leaving your business today, every day, while you wait for the next referral to arrive on its own schedule.

Referrals You Don't Control

Hospital discharge coordinators and placement agencies decide who hears about your home. When their priorities shift, your occupancy drops. You have no way to generate your own inquiries, and no way to predict what next month looks like.

Inquiries That Go Cold in Minutes

A family contacts several homes at once. Whoever follows up first gets the appointment, and usually gets the placement. If your intake relies on a callback later that day, you are losing inquiries you will never know existed.

Appointments That Don't Show Up

Even when a family agrees to visit, without a systematic confirmation and reminder process, no-shows are common. Your team's time, the family's trust, and your revenue expectation, all wasted when no one walks through the door.

Questions, Answered

Frequently Asked Questions

We have tried marketing agencies before and got leads we could not convert. How is this different?

That is the most common thing we hear, and it is a fair objection. Most agencies deliver a spreadsheet of names and call the job done. Some of those people were curious. Some were clicking ads at midnight with no real intent. None of them were followed up with within seconds. None of them had an appointment booked before the interest wore off. None of them received reminder sequences to make sure they actually showed up.

The gap is not the ads. It is everything that happens between the ad and the appointment. That is what we built our entire system around. Within seconds of a family submitting your inquiry form, they receive a warm text inviting them to book a visit. Within minutes, it's on your calendar. Reminders go out automatically. You get a booked, showed appointment. Not a name in a spreadsheet.

And then we guarantee it. If we don't hit the agreed number of showed appointments in 30 days, we refund our management fee in full. No dispute, no lengthy explanation needed. The agencies you worked with before did not offer that, because they could not back it up.

Is Meta advertising appropriate for care homes? Will it reach the right families?

Yes, and it is one of the most underused channels in this sector. The families making care decisions are adults between 45 and 70, and they are on Facebook and Instagram. Meta lets us target by location, life stage, and behavioral signals that indicate someone is actively researching care options. The ads we run do not feel like advertising; acknowledge the family's situation with warmth and direct them to learn more. The result is an inquiry from someone already in decision mode, not a cold click from a casual browser.

What do you actually do, and how much of my time does this take?

We handle everything on the advertising and booking side: writing and designing the ads, building the inquiry form, setting up the instant text-back, and connecting to your calendar. You do one onboarding call (roughly 60 minutes) where we learn about your home, your capacity, and what a good resident fit looks like. After that, your only job is to show up for the appointments we put on your calendar. We send a weekly report so you always know what is running and what it is producing.

How much do we need to spend on ads?

The minimum is $50–$100 per day ($1,500–$3,000/month), paid directly to Meta. You control the budget inside your own ad account. We never touch your money or mark it up. That spend level is what generates enough data to run the system properly and hit the appointment targets we guarantee.

To put it in perspective: your total monthly investment (management fee plus ad spend) is roughly $3,500–$5,000. One filled bed typically earns you $3,000–$8,000 per month. The math works the moment a single resident moves in.

Is this compliant? We operate in a regulated sector.

We do not make medical or clinical claims; that is your domain. Our ads speak to the family's search for a safe, caring home environment, not to clinical outcomes or quality assessments. We follow Meta's advertising policies for housing and care-related content, and we write all copy to be substantiated and accurate. Everything we run, you review and approve before it goes live.

What if you do not deliver the agreed number of appointments?

If we don't hit the agreed number of booked, showed resident appointments within 30 days, message us and we refund our management fee in full. No dispute, no long explanation needed.

Do you work with multiple care homes in the same area?

No. We only take on one home per geographic area. Once we're working with a home in your catchment, that area is closed to new clients. We do this because we're running targeted ads in your specific location. If we worked with two homes in the same area we would be splitting results and competing against ourselves. It also means the home we work with gets our full focus. First to book the strategy call secures the area.

How quickly can we get started?

After the strategy call and a signed agreement, we typically have the system built and the first ads running within 7 to 10 business days. The onboarding call is the main time commitment from your side; after that, we take over. The 30-day guarantee window starts the day the first ad goes live, not the day you sign.
