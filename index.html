The Brutal Reality

Why Most Ad Spend Never Turns Into A Booked Appointment

The problem isn't your market. It's the gap between a lead hitting submit and someone actually showing up. Every agency you've tried has ignored that gap.

The "Clicks & Impressions" Scam

Your agency sends a report full of reach, CTR, and impressions. Your calendar is empty. Those are activity metrics, not revenue metrics, and most agencies hide behind them because they can't promise anything harder.

The Lead Response Black Hole

A lead submits their info, then waits. Your team sees it hours later and calls. No answer. They try again. Voicemail. By then, that lead has already booked with whoever called first. The window is minutes, not hours.

The 7-Field Form Friction

Most lead forms ask for name, email, phone, address, how they heard about you, what service, preferred time, and a message. The prospect just wanted to inquire. Most bail before hitting submit, and the ones who do often aren't serious.

The No-Show Appointment

Even when a lead books, a manual process with no follow-up means they forget, change their mind, or double-book. No confirmation, no reminder, no show. You've paid for an ad that produced nothing.

How It Works

The Ad Engine: Why Each Step Exists

A lead cools off fast. The business that reaches them first, with the right message in the right moment, wins the appointment. Every step of this system is designed around that one truth.

01

The HookThe Scroll-Stopping Static Ad

Your potential customer is on their phone when the problem is real, right now, in this moment. We build a static image ad that calls out their exact situation directly, so they stop scrolling because they feel seen, not because the creative is flashy. That moment of recognition is what creates a qualified lead instead of a curious click.

02

The CaptureOne-Tap Lead Form

We pull their name and number from Meta's pre-filled form in a single tap. No typing, no seven fields, no friction. The shorter the ask, the more real people actually submit. Every extra field you add is a reason for a qualified prospect to abandon. We eliminate the reasons.

03

The Lock-InInstant Text-Back & Booking

The second a lead submits, an automated text goes out within seconds, not hours. It prompts them to pick a time slot directly from your calendar. They book while the intent is still hot. A confirmation goes out automatically. You get a booked, reminded appointment, not a name in a spreadsheet that your team has to chase.

Why static image ads instead of video?

Static ads load instantly and stop the scroll in under three seconds, before a video has even buffered. A sharp image with the right words does one thing: it makes your ideal customer think "that's exactly my problem," and click. High-production video introduces production delays, higher cost, and creative risk. We run what consistently produces the lowest cost per booked appointment, and right now that's a well-crafted static ad with problem-focused copy.

What if I don't hit the promised number of booked appointments?

If we don't hit the agreed number of booked, showed appointments within 30 days, and you've held up your end (maintained the minimum ad budget, completed onboarding, honored the appointments we booked), you message us and we issue a full refund of our management fee. We don't take the fee unless we earn it.
