# Scalevertise Loom Script: Demo Funnel Walkthrough

**Purpose:** Screen-recording you send to care home owners (after a call, or cold in a DM/email) to show them exactly what the system does. This is your process proof. You are not showing off a website. You are showing them the machine that fills their empty beds.

**Length target:** 3 to 4 minutes. Keep it moving.

**Tone:** Calm, confident, helpful. You are the expert walking them through something you have built many times. Talk like you are showing a friend, not pitching.

**Setup before you hit record:**
- Have `demo-carehome.html` open in a clean browser tab (no other tabs visible, bookmarks bar hidden).
- Have your face cam on in the corner. People buy from people.
- Fill the form out for real during the recording so they see the whole flow end to end.
- Use a believable name when you fill it in (not "test test").

---

## PHASE 1: THE HOOK (0:00 to 0:20)

*[Start on the landing page, top of the hero. Do not scroll yet.]*

> "Hey, it's Vincent from Scalevertise. I wanted to quickly show you the exact system we'd build to fill your empty beds, so instead of just telling you, I'm going to walk you through a live one right now.
>
> This is a demo home we built called Cedar Grove. Everything you're about to see is what your home would have, branded to you. Give me about three minutes."

*[Don't oversell here. The frame is "let me just show you."]*

---

## PHASE 2: THE LANDING PAGE (0:20 to 1:15)

*[Slowly scroll the hero. Let the video background play for a second.]*

> "So this is the page a family lands on the moment they click your ad. Notice what it does in the first three seconds.
>
> Right at the top: 'Only a few beds available this month.' That's urgency, and it's real. You DO have limited beds. That alone makes families act instead of bookmarking and forgetting.
>
> The headline speaks to them, not about you. 'A warm, caring home for the person who raised you.' That's the adult daughter or son who's up at midnight stressed about Mom. We're speaking directly to that feeling."

*[Scroll to the trust bar and proof strip.]*

> "Then immediately we build trust. State licensed, Google rating, family owned, we call back fast. These are the exact things a worried family is scanning for."

*[Scroll down through empathy, care types, why-us, testimonials briefly. Don't read every word.]*

> "As they scroll, we handle the emotional side. We show we understand what they're going through, we show the types of care, the proof, real stories from other families, and we answer the questions that stop people from picking up the phone. Cost, safety, 'what if they don't want to move.'
>
> Every section is built to do one thing: get them to book a visit."

---

## PHASE 3: THE FORM (1:15 to 2:00)

*[Scroll back up to the form. Start filling it in live.]*

> "Here's where it gets good. This is the form. And notice it's not some clunky contact form. Watch.
>
> Name, phone, email. Then instead of boring dropdowns, they just tap. 'This care is for my parent.' 'Assisted living.' And here's the important one: 'When do you need to make a decision?' I'll click 'within 30 days.'
>
> That last question is gold for you. It tells you who's urgent before you even pick up the phone, so you call the hot ones first."

*[Click submit. Let the "checking availability" spinner play.]*

> "Now they hit submit, and instead of a dead 'thanks, we'll be in touch' page that kills momentum, watch what happens."

---

## PHASE 4: THE BOOKING PAGE (2:00 to 2:50)

*[The booking page loads. Let them see the progress bar at top.]*

> "It takes them straight to booking. See the progress bar? Their details are done, now they pick a time. There's no waiting, no 'someone will call to schedule.' We capture the appointment while they're still motivated.
>
> They pick a day. Pick a time."

*[Click a day, then a time. Let the slots animate in.]*

> "And confirm."

*[Click confirm. Let the "Reserving your time" spinner and the animated checkmark play out.]*

> "And that's a booked, confirmed appointment on the calendar. Notice it even lets them add it straight to their own Google Calendar, so they actually show up.
>
> That right there is the difference. Most marketing gets you a list of names you have to chase. This gets you booked tours."

---

## PHASE 5: THE CLOSE (2:50 to 3:30)

*[Go back to your face cam, full screen if you can, or just talk over the confirmation page.]*

> "So that's the whole machine. We run the ads, we build that page branded to your home, and we set up the booking and the instant follow-up. You just show up to the tours.
>
> And the way we work is simple: we guarantee 20 to 30 qualified resident appointments in 30 days, or you don't pay our management fee. We only work with one home per area, so whoever moves first locks out the competition.
>
> If you want, I'll check whether your area is still open and we can map out what this looks like for your home. Just reply to this and let me know. Talk soon."

*[Stop recording. Don't ramble after the ask.]*

---

## QUICK REFERENCE: THE 5 BEATS

1. **Hook** (20s): "Let me just show you the system."
2. **Landing page** (55s): urgency, family-first headline, trust, emotional sections.
3. **Form** (45s): tap selectors, the "when do you need to decide" question = your priority list.
4. **Booking** (50s): straight to a confirmed appointment, add to calendar.
5. **Close** (40s): the guarantee, one home per area, soft ask.

## DO / DON'T

- **DO** fill the form out live. Seeing the flow work is the whole point.
- **DO** keep your energy calm and certain. You're the expert.
- **DON'T** read this word for word. Know the beats, talk naturally.
- **DON'T** apologize, hedge, or say "this is just a demo." Own it.
- **DON'T** go past 4 minutes. Tighter is better.
