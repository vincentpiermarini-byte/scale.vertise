# Scalevertise — Strategy Call Script

Built on Hormozi's Closing Playbook + care-home context.
Goal: 25%+ close rate. Your only job on the call is to diagnose their pain, show the system solves it, and give them the power to decide.

---

## BEFORE THE CALL

- Record every call (tell them upfront: "I record calls for coaching purposes, is that okay?")
- Have a calculator open — you'll use the empty-bed cost math live
- Have the survey answers in front of you so you already know their situation

---

## PHASE 1 — OPEN (2 min)

**Goal:** Set the frame. You're a consultant diagnosing a problem, not a salesperson pitching a product.

> "Hey [name], appreciate you taking the time. Quick bit of housekeeping — I record calls for coaching purposes, that okay with you? Great.
>
> So the way I run these is pretty simple. I'm going to ask you a few questions about your home and your current situation, you tell me honestly what's going on, and then I'll tell you honestly whether I think we can help and how. If it's not a fit I'll tell you that too. Sound good?"

*[Wait for yes. This frame flips the dynamic — you're the one deciding if THEY qualify, not the other way around.]*

---

## PHASE 2 — DIAGNOSE (8–10 min)

**Goal:** Get them to say out loud what empty beds are costing them. They need to feel the pain before the solution means anything. Ask, then shut up and listen.

**Occupancy:**
> "How many beds do you have total, and roughly how many are empty right now?"

**How long:**
> "How long have those beds been empty?"

**Current approach:**
> "What are you doing right now to fill them? Referrals, directories, anything like that?"

**What's not working:**
> "And how's that going? Is it predictable, or more hit and miss?"

**The cost calculation — do this live, out loud:**
> "So if a bed is, say, £[X] a week, and you've had [N] empty beds for [Y] months... that's roughly [do the math] you haven't collected. Does that number feel about right to you?"

*[Let them sit with that number. Don't rush past it. That's the whole reason they're on the call.]*

**Previous attempts:**
> "Have you tried paid advertising before? What happened?"

*[If burned before — acknowledge it, don't argue. "Totally makes sense you'd be cautious. Most of the homes we talk to have had that exact experience. That's actually why the guarantee exists."]*

---

## PHASE 3 — EDUCATE THE MECHANISM (5 min)

**Goal:** Show them the system so they understand WHY it works. Don't just say "we run ads." Walk them through the three steps.

> "So let me show you how this actually works, because it's pretty different from what most people have tried.
>
> Step one: we run targeted ads on Meta — Facebook and Instagram — specifically aimed at adult children and spouses who are actively searching for care options right now. Not everyone. The exact people in that moment.
>
> Step two: instead of sending them to a random website, they hit a short qualifying page that pre-screens them. So you're not getting calls from people who are just browsing — you're getting families who are genuinely ready to move.
>
> Step three: the moment someone submits, an automated text goes out within seconds inviting them to book a visit directly onto your calendar. They confirm while the intent is still there. You get booked, confirmed appointments — not a list of names to chase.
>
> The whole thing runs on your own ad account. You own it. We never touch your money. Ad spend goes straight to Meta."

---

## PHASE 4 — PRESENT THE OFFER (2 min)

**Goal:** Simple and direct. Don't undersell it, don't over-explain it.

> "Here's what we do for you. We build the whole system — the ads, the landing page, the follow-up automation — and we run it. Flat rate, £2,000 a month. You control the ad spend, that goes direct to Meta on your card.
>
> And we guarantee it: 20 to 30 qualified resident appointments in 30 days, or we refund our management fee. No argument, no paperwork. If we don't deliver, you don't pay us.
>
> One thing worth knowing — we only work with one home per catchment area. So if there's a competing home nearby, whoever gets in first has the exclusive. That's it."

---

## PHASE 5 — ASK FOR THE DECISION (1 min)

**Hormozi rule:** Only ask when you think you've got them. If you're not sure, keep probing.

> "Based on everything we've talked about — does this sound like something that makes sense for your home?"

*[Shut up. Wait. Let them respond. The first person to speak after the ask loses.]*

---

## PHASE 6 — HANDLE OBJECTIONS

### "I need to think about it" / "Let me think about it"

*This is avoidance, not a real objection. Draw out the real concern.*

> "Totally get it, this is a real decision. Can I ask — what's the main thing you'd be thinking about? What's the concern?"

*[They'll give you the real objection now. Work that.]*

If they're still vague:

> "Here's what I'd hate to happen. You go away, get busy, and the beds stay empty another month. At [£X/bed], that's [£Y] more gone. What would need to be true for this to be a yes?"

---

### "It's too expensive" / "£2,000 is a lot"

> "Totally understand it feels like a lot. Can I ask — what are you spending now to fill beds? Referral fees, directory listings, any of that?"

*[Usually the answer is inconsistent, variable, or nothing — which is worse.]*

> "So right now you've got [N] empty beds losing you roughly [£X] a month, and you're spending [£0 / £Y inconsistently] on filling them. We're saying £2,000 a month, guaranteed results, and if we don't hit the appointments target you don't pay us. At what point does the cost of NOT doing it become higher than the cost of doing it?"

---

### "I've been burned by agencies before"

*Acknowledge first. Never argue.*

> "That makes complete sense, and honestly I'd be surprised if you hadn't — most agencies in this space overpromise and underdeliver. Can I ask what happened specifically?"

*[Let them vent. Then:]*

> "So the difference here is simple. We don't take a retainer and hope for the best. The guarantee means if we don't hit the agreed number of appointments in 30 days, you don't pay our fee. The only way that makes sense for us is if we're confident it works. The agency that burned you — did they offer anything like that?"

---

### "I need to talk to my partner / board / manager"

> "Of course. Is it something you'd want to do together on a call? I'm happy to walk them through it the same way I have with you — that way they hear it directly and can ask questions. When are they usually available?"

*[Try to get a three-way call, not a "I'll relay what you said" situation. Information always degrades.]*

---

### "I want to wait until [time / season / when we're less busy]"

> "I hear that. Quick question — if you did wait until then, what changes? The beds would still be empty until you do something different.
>
> Also worth saying — we only take one home per area. I can't hold the spot. If another home in your catchment signs up in the meantime, that's the area gone."

---

### "I'm not sure it'll work for us specifically"

*Fear objection — they believe it works, just not for them.*

> "What makes you think your situation's different?"

*[Listen. Address specifically. Then:]*

> "Here's how I think about it. Best case — it works exactly as we described, beds fill, you've got a predictable system. Worst case — we don't hit the targets and you get your management fee back. Either way you're not worse off than you are right now with empty beds. The only guaranteed outcome of not doing it is the beds stay empty."

*(Hormozi "Best Case Worst Case" close)*

---

## PHASE 7 — CLOSE AND SHUT UP

When they say yes:

> "Great. Let me send you the agreement now while we're on the call. Takes two minutes. Once that's signed we'll book the onboarding call and get the build started."

**Hormozi rule: once they say yes, shut up. Don't keep selling. Stop talking.**

---

## THE ONE NUMBER TO HAVE READY

Empty beds × weekly rate × weeks empty = money already lost.

Do this calculation live on the call with their actual numbers. It makes the £2,000/month fee look small by comparison every time.

**Example:** 3 empty beds × £1,200/week × 8 weeks = £28,800 gone. "And we're talking about £2,000 a month to fix that."

---

## AFTER THE CALL

Send recording to agency for review within 24 hours. Ask specifically: "Where did I lose them?" or "What should I have said at [moment]?"

This is the highest-leverage hour you spend every week.
