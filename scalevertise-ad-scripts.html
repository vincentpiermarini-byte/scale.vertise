Context

Why Jeremy's Scripts Work

Before the scripts, here is what makes Jeremy's format effective and why we kept the same structure.

Our offer is actually stronger than Jeremy's. He offers 10 residents in 90 days. We offer 20-30 qualified appointments in 30 days. That means we can use the same proven script structure and lead with a more compelling result. The formula stays the same: call-out the owner directly, state exactly what you will do for them, give the specific result and timeframe, then attach the guarantee.
  • Leads with a direct call-out to care home owners, not a generic hook
  • States the exact result and timeframe upfront, no build-up
  • The guarantee removes all the risk before they can object
  • No jargon, no agency speak, sounds like a person talking
  • Short enough to hold attention, long enough to give proof
Section 01

UGC Hooks

These are the first 3-5 seconds. The job is to stop the scroll and make a care home owner feel like this is directly for them. Use one hook per ad. Test all three.

Hook 1 — Direct Offer

"If you're a care home owner, here's exactly what I'll do for you. I will install our Resident Acquisition System into your business and get you 20 to 30 qualified family tours booked in the next 30 days, or you don't pay our management fee. Simple as that."

Hook 2 — Problem First

"Everyone tells you the way to fill your care home is to post on social media, rely on word of mouth, and wait for GP referrals to come in. And maybe that worked years ago. But right now there are families searching online every single day for a care home in your area, and if you're not showing up in front of them with a real offer, someone else is."

Hook 3 — Credibility Lead

"We run paid ads specifically for care home owners, and right now we're getting 20 to 30 qualified family enquiries booked directly onto care home calendars every single month. I want to show you exactly how we do it, and why we're confident enough to guarantee it or you don't pay."
